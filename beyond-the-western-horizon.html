THE LONG WAY HOME · CAMPAIGN THREE

BEYOND THE WESTERN HORIZON.

A Family Circumnavigation

Explore the CampaignThe Full Journey

Set out together around the world.

The circumnavigation is the entire third campaign. Beyond the Western Horizon begins when we depart after final refit, outfitting, and provisioning. It brings together the family’s experience from Proving Grounds and The Patagonia Problem.

Family sailing, shared learning, and crew development continue throughout the voyage, with opportunities to explore and learn from the places we reach.

Prepare the family and the vessel.

Final preparation will be shaped by the selected cruiser, the crew’s experience, and the intended passages. The route, departure date, and detailed itinerary will develop with those decisions.

Funding, readiness, weather, health, vessel condition, and entry requirements will shape departure and passage planning.
