NAVIGATION & ENERGY

Extend the Catalina's capability.

Mission 1 begins the refit and develops experience aboard the Catalina. Mission 2 adds the navigation electronics and solar capacity required for longer, more independent operation.

I’ll use the Mexico voyage to test how the boat and upgraded systems work together, what needs attention, and which procedures need more practice. The Tequila Run gives the mission its name. Building reliable navigation and energy capability gives it its purpose.

UPGRADE. TEST. LEARN.

Capability has to work at sea.

Lessons from the Texas run will guide the Catalina’s next upgrades. Each stage identifies weaknesses and addresses them before the difficulty increases.

01 · REVIEW & ASSESS

Apply the Texas lessons

Review the Catalina’s performance, repairs, power use, and navigation needs after Mission 1. Confirm the work before selecting equipment.

02 · INSTALL & INTEGRATE

Upgrade navigation and solar

Add the selected electronics and solar system with attention to reliability, power balance, maintainability, and backup methods.

03 · TEST & PRACTICE

Learn the systems

Test the upgrades locally before the extended voyage. Build familiarity with operation, maintenance, failure modes, and backup procedures.

04 · VOYAGE & REVIEW

Take the lessons to sea

Use the Mexico voyage as the initial proving trip. Document how the boat, equipment, and procedures perform in real conditions. Repairs, upgrades, and lessons identified here feed directly into Mission 3’s offshore preparation.

MISSION 2 FUNDING

The additional Mission 2 allowance targets the Catalina’s navigation electronics and solar upgrades. Final equipment, installation scope, and spending will follow assessment and the lessons from Mission 1.
