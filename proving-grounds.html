THE LONG WAY HOME · CAMPAIGN ONE

PROVING GROUNDS.

Training, experience, and crew development through four missions. Build the skills that carry the family into the next adventures.

Explore the MissionsThe Full Journey

Build the crew through experience.

Proving Grounds turns preparation into practical experience. The first three missions retain their established voyage concepts and maps, building seamanship, navigation, offshore experience, and confidence.

The Anchoring Mission brings those lessons into a family voyage centered on exploration and learning ashore.

Four missions of development.

MISSION 1

Texas BBQ Run

Refit and learn Big Trouble, build practical seamanship, and make the first extended proving voyage to Texas. The target launch year is 2030.

Explore Texas BBQ Run

MISSION 2

Tequila Run

Develop navigation and energy systems, then test the boat and crew on the Mexico voyage.

Explore Tequila Run

MISSION 3

Bermuda Triangle Rum Run

Build offshore experience from Florida to Bermuda and the USVI, returning through the Caribbean Sea around western Cuba.

Explore Bermuda Triangle Rum Run

MISSION 4

Anchoring Mission

Cruise as a family for roughly one to two months, visiting about 10 anchorages chosen for learning and exploration. This mission requires the larger family cruiser.

Explore Anchoring Mission

A training boat and a family cruiser.

Big Trouble, our Catalina 36, is the training boat. She can serve on earlier missions when properly outfitted for the intended passage and crew. The Anchoring Mission is the first mission that requires a larger family cruiser, and our research into that boat is underway.

The Texas BBQ Run targets 2030.

We’re cutting expenses and taking on extra work to bring departure forward if we’re ready sooner. The Texas BBQ Run targets 2030. Funding targets will be revisited after we acquire the family cruiser and assess its needs.
