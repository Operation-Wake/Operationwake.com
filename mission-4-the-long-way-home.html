PROVING GROUNDS · MISSION FOUR

THE ANCHORING MISSION.

A family learning voyage. About 10 anchorages, one to two months, and time ashore to explore each place together.

Explore the MissionAll Proving Grounds Missions

Let each stop become a classroom.

The Anchoring Mission brings the family together aboard a larger cruiser for roughly one to two months. We’ll plan for about 10 anchorage destinations, spending a day or two at each when conditions and the itinerary allow.

Destinations will be chosen for what the children can explore and learn, including historical and cultural sites, maritime locations, and nature. Anchoring practice is part of the voyage as the family builds confidence living and traveling aboard.

Explore, learn, and share.

History and culture

Explore local sites and learn how people, communities, and trade have shaped the places we visit.

Maritime life

Visit maritime locations and connect navigation, weather, and seamanship with daily life aboard.

Nature

Explore preserves, shorelines, and habitats, observing the natural world as a family.

A record of learning

Give the children opportunities to record or share discoveries through notes, photographs, drawings, and conversations.

The first mission for the larger family cruiser.

We’re researching a larger cruiser that can support family life aboard and the voyages ahead. The Anchoring Mission begins that boat’s role in our family adventures, following inspection, refit, outfitting, and preparation for the crew and passage.

Family Cruiser Search

Set the route close to launch.

The final route and anchorage list will be selected close to launch. Each stop will be considered alongside a suitable alternate, passage distance and travel time, educational opportunities, planned stay, and weather flexibility.

The destination list and learning activities will grow around the places we select.

Carry the lessons into Patagonia.

Completing Proving Grounds prepares the family for the next campaign, The Patagonia Problem.
