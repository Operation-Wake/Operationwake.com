LEARN TOGETHER. GO FARTHER TOGETHER.

A family journey, earned one mission at a time.

The Patagonia Problem brings the family into the next Operation Wake challenge. Three train-up missions build toward a shared expedition down South America, through Patagonia, north to Mexico, and back through the Panama Canal.

The passages are only part of the story. Throughout the journey, planned time ashore will give us opportunities to explore, learn, and experience the places we reach together.

THE CREW

Prepare as a family

Build confidence, onboard routines, shared responsibilities, and the skills to travel together.

THE CLASSROOM

Learn along the way

Connect life aboard with geography, nature, history, and the communities we visit.

THE APPROACH

Grow our range

Use each train-up mission to learn what the family and vessel need before the next journey.

THREE TRAIN-UP MISSIONS. ONE FAMILY EXPEDITION.

Start close. Build confidence. Head south.

Four missions form the challenge. Each journey adds experience before the final expedition.

MISSION 01 · FAMILY SHAKEDOWN

Dry Tortugas & Key West

Home → Dry Tortugas → Key West → home.

Begin with a shared adventure to the Dry Tortugas, continue to Key West, and return home. Develop the everyday routines of sailing and exploring as a family.

MISSION 02 · ISLAND EXPLORATION

Explore the Bahamas

Home → the Bahamas → home.

Spend time exploring the Bahamas together. Extend our family cruising experience and make room for learning and discovery ashore. Individual islands and stops are still to be selected.

MISSION 03 · FAMILY & OFFSHORE MILES

Grandma, Bermuda & the USVI

Home → Maine → Bermuda → U.S. Virgin Islands → home.

Visit Grandma in Maine, then continue to Bermuda and the U.S. Virgin Islands before returning home. Build experience with longer passages and extended time aboard together.

MISSION 04 · THE FINAL EXPEDITION

The Patagonia Problem

South America → Patagonia → Mexico → Panama Canal → home.

Bring the lessons of the first three missions into a family expedition down South America, through Patagonia, and north to Mexico, with a return through the Panama Canal. Include stops ashore to explore and learn throughout the trip.

THE FAMILY EXPEDITION VESSEL

The Right Boat for the Family

Acquiring a different boat is a serious possibility for The Patagonia Problem, with a cruising catamaran among the options under consideration. The selected vessel will need to support family life aboard, the three train-up missions, and the final expedition through Patagonia.

The boat type, purchase budget, and required refit remain under development. Any candidate will be evaluated for offshore capability, condition, carrying capacity, family accommodations, and suitability for the intended route.
